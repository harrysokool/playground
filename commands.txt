Questions for the business auditors
Area	Questions to ask
1. Information extraction	Which fields do you normally check? Doctor, patient, registration number, date, receipt number, amount, or anything else?
2. Cross-checking	Do you compare receipt details with claim submissions, policy information, doctor registries or other systems?
3. Medical information	Do you check diagnoses, symptoms, treatments, medication details or whether a diagnosis comment exists?
4. Financial checks	Do you verify the total, individual charges, discounts, duplicate receipts or unusual amounts?
5. Doctor verification	Do you only confirm that the doctor is registered, or do you need to identify the actual treating doctor?
6. Exceptions	What are the most common mistakes or suspicious cases you encounter?
7. Decision making	Which checks are straightforward rule based comparisons, and which require human judgment or context?
8. Workload	How many receipts are reviewed, how long does each take, and which checks consume the most time?
