Summary
The existing business rules for in-force (00/10) and terminated (51/61/62/70/81) status codes are verified correct and complete — no additional codes exist beyond these seven. However, two important caveats emerged: one code (62) has a mislabeled description, and a small number of "in-force" Agency/PD records unexpectedly carry a populated termination date, which is a data inconsistency worth flagging rather than acting on.

2. Active/terminated definition check
Verified: the existing rule is correct and exhaustive.
Codes 00 and 10 → in-force: confirmed present and consistently labeled "Inforce" variants across all three row types.
Codes 51, 61, 62, 70, 81 → terminated: confirmed present (though 62 and 81 do not occur for PD-TR, and 70/81 do not occur for PD) and consistently labeled "Terminated" variants.
No additional, unexpected, or unmapped status codes exist in any row_type — the full code inventory for this table is exactly {00, 10, 51, 61, 62, 70, 81}.

Verified facts:
123 in-force (00) Agency agents carry a non-trivial suspension indicator, confirming agent_status_code and suspend_code are independent fields, as instructed.
suspend_effective_date/suspend_expiry_date are populated for only a small minority of records with a suspension indicator (e.g., 12 of 123 in-force+suspended Agency rows) — most suspension-indicator rows have no date information at all, and none of the populated expiry dates have already passed. This means suspend dates cannot be relied on to determine whether a suspension is "currently active" for the large majority of flagged records — the date fields are too sparse to support a date-based "currently suspended" rule. A populated, non-00 suspend_code is the only consistently available signal, and even that — per existing guidance — indicates a suspension/special-handling indicator, not confirmed current suspension status.

Verified facts:
Every terminated record (100%) has a termination_effective_date populated — fully consistent with agent_status_code.
A data inconsistency exists: 596 Agency and 47 PD records are coded as currently in-force (00/10) yet still carry a populated termination_effective_date, with dates ranging as far back as 1980 and as recent as 2026-10-16 6. This cannot be explained from available fields — it may reflect reinstated agents whose historical termination date was never cleared, a reporting lag, or a data-quality defect. This is flagged as unresolved and should not be used to override agent_status_code when determining active/terminated status; agent_status_code remains the authoritative field.
status_change_date is far less consistently populated than termination_effective_date (only 61.2% of terminated Agency rows, 18.2% of terminated PD rows) and should not be relied upon as a complete source for status-change timing.

Per instructions, their business meaning is not inferred from the data. What is verified is only that: (a) only two values exist, T and I; (b) neither value aligns exclusively with in-force or terminated agent_status_code — both occur under both; (c) T and I are therefore independent of agent_status_code and cannot be used as a proxy for it.
