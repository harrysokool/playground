**4. Overlaps and conflicts**
- **No duplicates:** the new licence measures cover all row types, while the existing ones are limited to Agency or PD, so they don't repeat each other.
- **Names close to the filters:** "Number of active Agency agents" and "Active Agency agents" are a measure and a filter. They use the same logic, so they don't conflict.
- **`COUNT(*)` for education records:** this is correct only when the education table is the only table in the query, or is counted before any join. Joining to licence records would multiply the rows, and the title says so. You may prefer counting distinct combinations of `INTAGTSEQ` and `agt_edu_seq` here, since that stays correct after joins; I kept your expression.
- **Join warning on the licence-record measures:** the three licence-record counts that use COUNT(…THEN 1) can also be multiplied by joins. Only the new one has that warning in its title; the two existing Agency and PD ones were left as they were.
- **Not created, as you asked:** measures for currently suspended agents, technical representative headcount, or education verification success rates.
