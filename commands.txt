## 1. Agency measures

Table: gold_agent_master

Total Agency agents:
COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN row_type = 'Agency' THEN INTAGTSEQ END)

Active Agency agents:
COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN row_type = 'Agency' AND agent_status_code IN ('00', '10') THEN INTAGTSEQ END)

Terminated Agency agents:
COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN row_type = 'Agency' AND agent_status_code IN ('51', '61', '62', '70', '81') THEN INTAGTSEQ END)

## 4. Licence measures

Table: gold_agent_license

Currently effective typed licence records:
COUNT(CASE WHEN license_type IS NOT NULL AND license_eff_date IS NOT NULL AND license_exp_date IS NOT NULL AND license_eff_date <= CURRENT_DATE() AND license_exp_date >= CURRENT_DATE() THEN 1 END)

Distinct currently effective licence types:
COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN license_type IS NOT NULL AND license_eff_date IS NOT NULL AND license_exp_date IS NOT NULL AND license_eff_date <= CURRENT_DATE() AND license_exp_date >= CURRENT_DATE() THEN license_type END)

Important:

These measures use date validity only.

The license_type field includes licences, training, certifications, sales eligibility, and restriction records.

Do not describe these measures as counts of regulatory authorizations.

Review the existing Agency and PD licence type measures. Preserve them if their calculations are correct. If changes are needed, explain the difference before replacing them.

## 6. Configuration rules

1. Use valid Databricks SQL measure expressions.
2. Use table-qualified columns where required.
3. Check existing measures before adding new ones.
4. Do not overwrite correct measures.
5. Avoid creating duplicate measures with different names.
6. Clearly distinguish agents, firms, technical representative records, licence records, licence types, and education records.
7. Ensure entity counts use DISTINCT to prevent double counting after joins.
8. For licence and education record measures, warn that joining multiple tables can multiply rows. Use suitable aggregation or EXISTS when necessary.
9. Do not define measures for currently suspended agents, unique technical representative headcount, or education verification success rates because their business definitions are not confirmed.
10. Do not assume every record with a currently effective licence date is a valid regulatory authorization.
