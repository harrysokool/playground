5. Currently effective licence records

Table: gold_agent_license

SQL:
license_type IS NOT NULL
AND license_eff_date IS NOT NULL
AND license_exp_date IS NOT NULL
AND license_eff_date <= CURRENT_DATE()
AND license_exp_date >= CURRENT_DATE()

Synonyms: currently effective licence records, valid licence periods, unexpired licence records

Usage guidance:

This fifth filter checks date validity only. It does not confirm regulatory authorization because license_type includes training, certifications, eligibility flags, and restrictions.

Do not assume that every effective licence record represents permission to sell insurance.
