TASK 1: Table descriptions

Configure accurate descriptions for all three tables.

gold_agent_master:
Contains Agency agents, Partnership Distribution firms, and PD technical representative records. Includes entity identifiers, status, channels, contract information, suspension indicators, and termination details. Agency and PD rows represent unique agents or firms. Technical representative rows have a different record grain and must not automatically be counted as unique individuals.

gold_agent_license:
Contains regulatory licences, training, examinations, certifications, product eligibility, and restriction records. Each row describes a type code and its effective or expiry period. Multiple records may exist per entity. Not every record represents a regulatory licence.

gold_agent_education:
Contains educational qualifications and verification information for Agency agents only. Each row represents one education entry, uniquely identified by the combination of INTAGTSEQ and agt_edu_seq. Agents can have multiple entries.
