## Relationship 1: Agency and PD master to licence

Left table: gold_agent_master
Right table: gold_agent_license

Required conditions:

master.INTAGTSEQ = license.INTAGTSEQ
AND master.row_type = license.row_type
AND master.row_type IN ('Agency', 'PD')

Relationship type: One master record to many licence records.

INTAGTSEQ is unique within Agency and PD master records, but multiple licence records may exist for each entity.

Do not join using INTAGTSEQ alone without matching row_type.

## Relationship 2: PD technical representative master to licence

Left table: gold_agent_master
Right table: gold_agent_license

Required conditions:

master.INTAGTSEQ = license.INTAGTSEQ
AND master.row_type = 'PD-TR'
AND license.row_type = 'PD-TR'
AND master.pd_tr_INTTRAGTSEQ = license.pd_tr_INTTRAGTSEQ

Relationship type: One master technical representative record to many licence records.

INTAGTSEQ identifies the parent firm, not the technical representative.

pd_tr_INTTRAGTSEQ identifies the technical representative record and must be included in this join.

Previously verified results:

36,815 master PD-TR records.
34,799 master records have matching licence records.
2,016 master records have no matching licence records.
All 78,948 PD-TR licence rows match master records.

## Relationship 3: Agency master to education

Left table: gold_agent_master
Right table: gold_agent_education

Required conditions:

master.INTAGTSEQ = education.INTAGTSEQ
AND master.row_type = 'Agency'

Relationship type: One Agency agent to many education records.

Previously verified results:

144,509 education records.
136,467 distinct Agency agents with education records.
All education records match Agency master records.
No education records belong to PD firms or PD technical representatives.

Do not join education to PD or PD-TR records.
