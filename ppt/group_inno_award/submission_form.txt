Innovation Idea Awards - Application Form
The AIA Innovation Idea Awards aims to harness the impactful and creative ideas of AIA employees across 18 local markets to drive and accelerate innovation in the insurance industry. All AIA staff across the 18 markets can join and submit their innovative ideas across the three categories on how we can achieve our Triple Double Vision: 1) Double Customer Value, 2) Double Distribution Value, and 3) Double Employee Value. The application form should include a detailed written narrative explaining the innovation idea and any supplementary materials such as PowerPoint slides, Excel file, and/or videos. The submission deadline is September 30, 2026 at 5pm HKT If you have any questions, please contact the Group Innovation Office (aia.group.innovation.office@aia.com).
