Dear Colleagues,
 
We are pleased to launch the 2026 Innovation Impact Awards and Innovation Idea Awards under this year’s theme: Innovation for Enduring Value.
<br>All AIA employees are eligible for these awards. They are designed to recognise the innovation achievements we have already delivered across AIA, and the bold ideas that can help shape our future.
 
<ul><li>The Innovation Impact Awards celebrate successful innovation projects delivered over the past year, highlighting leading practices, measurable business impact and scalable solutions across our markets.</li></ul> 
<ul><li>The Innovation Idea Awards invite all AIA employees to submit creative ideas that can improve how we work, drive innovation in insurance and create enduring value for our customers, distribution partners, employees and business.</li></ul> 
Whether you have delivered a project with proven impact or have a new idea that could unlock future value, this is your opportunity to showcase your contribution, gain visibility with senior leaders and help build a stronger culture of innovation across AIA.
Innovation Impact Awards
 
These awards recognise innovation projects and solutions implemented between 30 September 2025 and 30 September 2026.<br><br>There are six award categories:
<br>
Innovation<br>Leader Award
Recognises the market that demonstrates overall leadership and best practices in innovation and AI.
 
<br>
Best Innovation<br>Adoption Award
Recognises the market that has achieved the strongest adoption of innovation and AI solutions.
 
<br>
Best Customer<br>Innovation Award
Recognises innovations that have delivered meaningful improvements to customer experience and outcomes.
<br>
Best Distribution<br>Innovation Award
Recognises innovations that have enhanced agent productivity, effectiveness or the broader distribution channel experience.
 
<br>
Best Operations<br>Innovation Award
Recognises innovations that have streamlined processes, increased efficiency or improved operational performance.
 
<br>
Best Employee<br>Value Award
Recognises innovations that improve the employee experience, productivity or ways of working across support functions.
To ensure a fair comparison, submissions will be assessed within two leagues:
 
<ul><li>Champions League: Group Office, AIA China, AIA Hong Kong, AIA Thailand, AIA Singapore, AIA Malaysia, Tata AIA</li><li>Premier League: All other markets</li></ul><br>Each award category will recognise gold, silver and bronze winners in both leagues.

Judging criteria
 
Submissions will be assessed based on four key criteria:
<br>
<br><br>
 
IMPACT - 50%
 
<br><br>
 
CREATIVITY - 30%
The extent to which the innovation creates meaningful business value, including customer, distribution, operational, employee or financial impact.
 
The originality and uniqueness of the idea or solution, particularly where it introduces new thinking or approaches not widely adopted before.
<br><br>
 
SCALABILITY - 10%
 
<br><br>
 
FEASIBILITY - 10%
The potential for the solution to be scaled or replicated across functions, business units or markets.
 
The practicality, cost, effort and ease of implementation required to bring the idea or solution to life.
For the Innovation Idea Awards, these criteria will be used to assess the proposal and final pitch. For the Innovation Impact Awards, the same principles will apply to applicable qualitative submissions, while selected categories will also leverage quantitative performance metrics tracked by the Group Innovation Office.
<br>Great ideas and impactful solutions can come from anywhere. We look forward to seeing your submissions and celebrating the people and teams shaping the future of innovation at AIA.
